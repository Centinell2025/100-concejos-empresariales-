# Distribution and intellectual-property review — Beacon Business

Prepared November 22, 2026. This is a release-preparation record, not a permit, legal opinion or certificate of copyright registration.

## Territory instructions

The owner selected **United States, Spain and Canada only**. Other territories must not be selected in Play Console. No store-country setting has been applied yet. Language choice does not change the approved territory list. The default language is English.

## Product reviewed

Beacon Business 1.0.0 is an offline business organizer with manually entered cash records, contacts, projects, tasks and educational tips. It has no bank connection, custom encryption, intrusion, vulnerability scanning, digital-forensics extraction, military or autonomous functions. It uses the standard Android WebView and Android document picker. These statements describe this build only and do not classify the owner’s other software.
