# Beacon Business

**Default listing language:** English (United States)

**App name:** Beacon Business

**Short description:** Track cash KPIs, income, expenses, clients, projects and tasks offline.

## Full description

See your business more clearly with Beacon Business, an offline workspace for small businesses, entrepreneurs and independent professionals.

GET STARTED WITH CONFIDENCE
A built-in introduction explains what to enter, where to work and how to read your indicators, with practical steps and a cash-flow example.

KEEP TRACK OF CASH
Record income received and expenses paid. View monthly cash totals, net cash results and cash margin, plus a six-month chart and a table of the underlying amounts. All financial entries use US dollars (USD).

ORGANIZE YOUR BUSINESS
Maintain client records, track potential opportunities, organize projects with planned budgets and deadlines, and manage tasks with priorities and completion status. Link tasks to projects and see your progress.

TURN IDEAS INTO ACTION
Explore 100 business tips covering strategy, customers, sales, finance, marketing, operations, leadership, innovation, technology and growth. Save favorites, add personal notes and complete practical actions.

CHOOSE YOUR LANGUAGE
English is the first-launch language. Spanish and Italian are available from the language selector. Changing the language keeps your records and personal notes.

OFFLINE BY DESIGN
No account, ads or subscriptions are required. Business records are stored locally on your device. Export a JSON copy through Android’s document picker to a location you choose. Exported files are not encrypted by the app.

The app does not connect to banks, automatically import transactions or sync records between devices. Restore a compatible Beacon JSON backup after reviewing its record counts, with an option to undo the most recent restoration on this device. Export monthly transactions as CSV. Set monthly income targets and expense budgets. The cash indicators are based on your manually recorded receipts and payments and are not accrual accounting, tax filing or personalized financial advice.
