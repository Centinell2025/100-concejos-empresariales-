# Beacon Business — Privacy Policy

Beacon of the Eagle LLC · Effective November 22, 2026 · Android versions 1.0.0–1.0.2



English

Beacon Business is an offline business organization application provided by Beacon of the Eagle LLC. It does not require an account.

Information you enter. You may enter business transactions, contact names, email addresses, phone numbers, project details, tasks and notes. These records, your progress and your language preference are stored locally in the app’s private WebView storage on your device. They are not sent to us.

No collection or sharing by the developer. The Android app has no Internet permission, advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs or cloud synchronization. We do not receive, collect, sell or share the information you enter. Core features work offline. The app does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photo library.

Exports. When you choose Export, Android asks you where to save a JSON backup or CSV report. A file you choose to place in another provider, such as cloud storage, is handled according to that provider’s practices. Exported JSON and CSV files are not encrypted by this app; keep it in a location you trust.

Restoring a backup. You may choose a compatible JSON file through Android’s document picker. The app validates it locally and shows a review before replacing records. A previous-state copy is kept locally so you can undo the most recent restoration; clearing app storage removes this copy too. Backup files are not sent to us.

Retention and deletion. Records remain on the device until you remove them or clear the app’s storage. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local records. Copies you exported remain wherever you saved them and must be removed separately. Automatic Android backup is disabled by the app. Individual deletion offers a short undo period in the app.

Your control. You decide which information to enter and export. Only enter contact or business data you are authorized to use. The app is intended for business users and is not designed for children.

Contact and updates. For privacy questions, contact centinellsuite@gmail.com. Changes to this policy will be reflected in an updated effective date. This policy describes the Android app; the separate hosted website is subject to the hosting service’s account and access practices.
